# Studium
An education platform designed for Indian schools. 

## Details
Studium connects teachers and students through a two-sided system: teachers upload their syllabus and exam timetable, and students get personalized study content generated by AI that stays aligned to what their actual teacher is teaching.

## Status

Currently in early development. v0.1 targeted for end of September 2026.

## Vision

To bridge the gap between the classroom and home study by giving students content aligned to their actual teacher's curriculum, while giving teachers per-student visibility into who understands what.
